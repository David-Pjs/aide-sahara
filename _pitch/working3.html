The problem

The web was built for eyes. Money most of all.

Sign-up forms, dashboards, bank apps. Almost all of it assumes you can see a screen and tap a target, with accessibility bolted on afterward, if at all.
It's not one broken step. A survey of ten major Nigerian banking apps by the Association for the Blind in Nigeria found most unusable for blind users altogether: unlabeled buttons, dead navigation, and OTP tokens that were never made speech-enabled in the first place.

The loop: poverty drives avoidable blindness through lack of access to care, and blindness then locks you out of earning, which deepens the poverty. The people the OTP shuts out of digital work are disproportionately the people who most need the work.
Aide breaks the loop at the earning step.

Business viability

Free to join. Four ways to earn. Nobody pays before they earn.

Transaction fee
10% blended, split 5% employer and 5% worker. Deducted only from completed gigs. Free for the first six months to build liquidity.
Managed hiring
Our margin engine. Employers pay Aide to run the whole selection process: source, assess, shortlist. ₦25,000 to ₦40,000 per hire, or ₦150,000/month unlimited for volume hirers. Transaction fee waived for these customers.
Direct reach
Employers pay to message verified candidates directly, instead of waiting on applications.
Aide Plus
₦500/month, optional, for workers: direct messaging to employers and priority matching. Applying stays free and unlimited for everyone. Free for anyone who completes a verified assessment.
Why it works
We don't tax hiring, we do the hiring, and screening is the expensive part employers gladly hand off. Workers never pay a signup fee, an application cost, or a withdrawal charge. They only ever pay out of money they've already earned.
How it's built

Every naira Aide speaks is real.

Receive
A real account, per worker
Each user gets their own dedicated Monnify bank account to be paid into.
Confirm
Counted only when paid
The balance is the sum of payments Monnify has confirmed, never an optimistic guess.
Verify
The name, checked first
Before a kobo moves, Aide reads back the real destination account name.
Pay out
A real transfer, by voice
Withdrawals run as live disbursements, authorized by a spoken phrase.
Aide is built on Monnify end to end: receive, confirm, verify, pay. The money is never a demo number.
How it's built

Designed from the need out, not retrofitted.

Atkinson Hyperlegible

Commissioned by the Braille Institute. It exaggerates the differences between the glyphs low-vision readers confuse (I/l/1, O/0, b/d), which matters most for account numbers and amounts.

AaIl1O0bd

Okabe–Ito, AAA contrast

The colorblind-safe standard palette, every foreground ≥ 7:1 on paper. No red/green pair ever carries meaning on its own.

Body text 15.8:1 · Actions 7.3:1

Never color alone

Every status is also stated in text and shape, so nothing is lost to color blindness or a screen reader.

Large by default

18px base, 48px targets, warm off-white ground that reduces the glare which triggers photophobia, with no zoom control to hunt for.
